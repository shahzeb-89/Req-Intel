Software Requirements Specification
System: Hospital Appointment Management System v1.0

1. The system shall allow patients to book appointments easily.
2. The system shall notify doctors about upcoming appointments in a timely manner.
3. The system shall load patient records fast.
4. The system shall be secure and protect sensitive medical data.
5. The system shall support a reasonable number of simultaneous users.
6. The system shall allow admin to manage doctor schedules efficiently.
7. The system shall generate appointment summaries for doctors.
8. The system shall provide appropriate error messages to users.
9. The system shall be compatible with modern browsers.
10. The system shall store patient history accurately.
11. The system shall allow patients to cancel appointments conveniently.
12. The system shall perform well under normal operating conditions.
13. The system shall send reminders to patients before their appointment.
14. The system shall allow doctors to update patient records.
15. The system shall be maintainable and scalable for future use.
