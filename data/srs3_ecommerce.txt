Software Requirements Specification
System: E-Commerce Platform v2.0

1. The system shall allow registered customers to add products to their shopping cart.
2. The system shall process payment transactions within 30 seconds under normal load.
3. The system shall send order confirmation emails to customers after successful purchase.
4. The system shall be fast and responsive at all times.
5. The system shall support a maximum of 1000 concurrent users during peak hours.
6. The system shall provide good search functionality for products.
7. The system shall encrypt all payment data using AES-256 encryption standard.
8. The system shall allow admin users to update product inventory in real time.
9. The system shall generate sales reports within 5 minutes for the last 30 days.
10. The system shall be easy to navigate for all types of users.
11. The system shall maintain a minimum uptime of 99.9% annually.
12. The system shall send low stock alerts to admin when product quantity falls below 10 units.
13. The system shall load product images efficiently on all device types.
14. The system shall allow customers to track their orders in real time.
15. The system shall provide appropriate product recommendations based on purchase history.
16. The system shall store customer data in compliance with GDPR regulations.
17. The system shall allow customers to apply discount codes during checkout.
18. The system shall handle large file uploads for product images up to 10MB.
19. The system shall display clear error messages when payment fails.
20. The system shall be scalable to support future growth.
